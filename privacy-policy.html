Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 28, 2026

Welcome to Davilanco Store ("we", "us", "our") - https://shop.davilanco.com/. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, shop with us, or contact us on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Your full name, phone number, email address, delivery address, state, city.
  • Order Information: Products you order, quantity, size, payment method, delivery details.
  • Device Information: Device type, browser, IP address, pages you visit on our site.
  • Communication: Messages you send us on WhatsApp, Instagram DM, email or contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Confirm payment and send order updates
  • Respond to your questions and complaints
  • Improve our store, products and customer service
  • Send you offers, new arrivals and promotions (only if you agree)
  • Prevent fraud and protect our store

3. How We Share Your Information

We do NOT sell your personal information. We only share it when necessary with:

  • Delivery companies / riders - only your name, phone number and delivery address, so they can deliver your order.
  • Payment providers - like Paystack, Flutterwave, Opay, bank transfer verification, to confirm your payment.
  • Law enforcement - only if required by Nigerian law.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to remember your cart, keep you logged in, and see which products people like. You can turn off cookies in your browser settings.

5. How We Protect Your Data

We protect your data with secure passwords, limited access, and secure payment links. We never ask you to send your card PIN, OTP, or bank password. If anyone asks you for OTP in our name, it is a scam. Report it to us immediately on WhatsApp: +2348032465205.

6. Your Rights

  • Ask what information we have about you
  • Ask us to correct wrong information
  • Ask us to delete your information
  • Stop receiving promotional messages from us

7. Children's Privacy

Our store is for people aged 18 and above, or minors buying with a parent/guardian's permission.

8. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
